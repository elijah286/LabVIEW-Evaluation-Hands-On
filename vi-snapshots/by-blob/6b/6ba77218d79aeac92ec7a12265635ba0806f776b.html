FIR filter specifications

<strong>FIR filter specifications</strong> are the minimum values needed to specify the FIR filter.

    		Topology

    <strong>Topology</strong> determines the design type of the filter.

    		Type

    <strong>Type</strong> specifies the passband of the filter according to the following values.

    		#Taps

    <strong>#Taps</strong> is the number of taps in the FIR filter. The default is 50.

    		Lower PB

    <strong>Lower PB</strong> is the lower of the two passband frequencies. The default is 100 Hz.

    		Upper PB

    <strong>Upper PB</strong> is the higher of the two passband frequencies. The default is 0.

    		Lower SB

    <strong>Lower SB</strong> is the lower of the two stopband frequencies. The default is 200 Hz.

    		Upper SB

    <strong>Upper SB</strong> is the higher of the two stopband frequencies. The default is 0.

window

window is the time-domain window to be used. It must be one of the following values:

- RECTANGLE (0)

- HANNING (1)

- HAMMING (2)

- BLKHARRIS (3)

- EXBLKMAN (4)

- BLKMAN (5)

- FLATTOP (6)

- BH4TERM (7)

- BH7TERM (8)

- LOWSIDELB (9)

- BLKMANNUTTALL (11)

- TRIANGLE (30)

- KAISER (60)

- DOLCHEBYSHEV (61)

- GAUSSIAN (62)

optional FIR filter specifications

<strong>optional FIR filter specifications</strong> is a cluster of additional parameters that can be used when specifying an FIR filter.

    		PB Gain

    <strong>PB Gain</strong> is the gain at the passband frequencies. Gain may be specified in linear terms or dB. The default is <span class="U+8211">-</span>3dB.

    		SB Gain

    <strong>SB Gain</strong> is the gain at the stopband frequencies. Gain may be specified in linear terms or dB. The default is <span class="U+8211">-</span>60dB.

    		Scale

    <strong>Scale</strong> determines how the <strong>PB Gain</strong> and <strong>SB Gain</strong> parameters are interpreted.

    		Window

    <strong>Window</strong> controls which smoothing window is applied to the truncated coefficients.
